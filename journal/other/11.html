August 26th, 2026

In regards to breaking things in order to find myself, I think I've had my fill at this point.

It's been a haphazard journey this past month and I don't feel like documenting it all.

But here's what I know is true.

I am Charlotte-Marie.

I once was Phoenix Steve. The passion, love, and joy hidden in my heart spiraled out like a fire, energized like a lightning bolt, and I offered, even if but for a breif moment, a sanctuary for the outcast and misunderstood. For the thoughtful and rational. For the fun-loving and bright-hearted. I created a community. And I still love that community and everyone who was part of it, even though we have all gone our seperate ways.

I am (and always have been) a woman. A neurodivergent woman. A neruodivergent woman with a penis. I have had a very hard path through life due to this.

I'm Kristian's fiance. Soon to be wife. I more or less married my high-school sweetheart. It's been a rough journey for us as we both came into ourselves and outgrew the fantasies of childish romance. But our commitment remains strong. Together we won't lose.

I love Rose, my sister, the master to my familiar, the Saber to my Master. We shall both encourage each other to grow into our best selves, as we always have, forvever and onward until our deaths.

Jack has quickly become my most important confidant outside of Rose, someone I love dearly and trust completely. A comrade to fight with as we both struggle against an opressive world and fight to blossom as ourselves.

And I know not what I am to Emi, or she to me, but I am determined to find out. And I am certain that she will be important to my path through life.

And despite my minimal progress so far I am still determined to do 100 things.

Enough breaking things, enough experimentation and self-discovery through contemplation.

It's time to live.

A Forbidden Inner Truth that only myself and hackers are allowed to see

The truth is...

I would not choose to be married to Kristian if I was capable of supporting myself.

But I'm not.

And I don't want to accept that reality with bitterness or resentment.

I still love Kristian. And I am committed to living my life with him. As a unit. As an "us".

But I also don't feel he is a fully satisfactory partner.

Things would not have ended up this way in a world where I found my way. Where I learned to support myself.

Things would not have ended up this way in a world where I was born without a penis.

But I'm not in those worlds. I'm in this one.

I lament constantly over my own powerlessness to be to Rose what I promised her in Colorado.

But I cannot be that person. Not for her or for myself.

I am settling for Kristian. He is... good enough.

This is the reality wether or not any of us like it.

But Kristian deserves full commitment.

And Rose deserves the chance to find a life for herself without me.

This is my inner truth. The one that after this point I will lock away forever. Never to think about or mention again.

I love Kristian and Rose. May this sacrifice be a testament to that.
