Example of High School Self

June 18th, 2026

So I originally wrote this with the intention of making it a story or vn or something, I'm not sure. The name is _ because I didn't want to use my real name, but the speaker is transparently me to such a degree that it's funny I even tried to obfuscate it at all. Eventually I just considered it an "example of my high school self" because it was directly based off of my memories of walking to first period in senior year. Steve was based off of Alex, and (()) was meant to be a combination of Victra and Pixel. Everything written is more or less actual things that happened, at least in my mind. I sometimes forget how bad it was. Real, true, ACTUAL self-hatred is something that only a limited few actually experience I've come to learn.

December 27th, 2015

Story Idea?

____. Thats my name.

Except usually I go by "__-y" because ___ is too common and I don't want to share a name with so many other people.

I have to be fucking UNIQUE, you know.? I just HAVE to be different. Have to be special.

God I really fucking hate myself. And I hate myself for hating myself.

What a glorious fucking cycle of self-hatred.

It's so fantastic, like I even realise how absurd it is, but that just makes me hate myself even more!

Man, I'm so fucking fantastic! God I just LOVE it! AHAHAHAHAH!

God. Fucking christ, like what the fuck, is that necessary?

Just shut up, shut up, shut up, shut up, shut up!

____ - ARGHH!

I emit a small nearly silent yell of maniacal frustration, even though I'm in the middle of public.

The people nearby probably think I'm a freak. Whatever. I don't care what they think.

But, clearly by making such a big deal about it I actually do care what they think. AHRGH! But who cares?! They're all worthless, anyway.

I imagine myself projecting a dark aura around me, swallowing and killing everyone around me, as I walk to class.

I spot my best friend Steve in front of me. Should I call out to him?

Why does it matter? It's not like it will mean anything if I go up and talk to him, I'm about to see him in class anyway.

I imagine focusing my dark aura at him and swallow him up too.

____ - Hahh.

God I'm such a fucking JOY. ARGH!

I mentally throw a chair in my head.

Whatever. I open the door to the school and walk to the classroom.

When I get to the classroom I notice that Steve has yet to arrive. Guess he stopped at the library or something first.

I sit down at my desk and bring out my laptop, opening up the chat client that my friends and I use.

(()) - G'mornin to ya.

Nonarc - Morning.

(()) - How be you, Nonarc?

Nonarc - Pretty decent.

Actually I feel fucking terrible, but it's not like I'm going to tell you that.

(()) - That's good.

Hahahahahaa. Yeah it's fucking great! Just fucking awesome being me. God how I want to just tell him that I hate him and wish everyone would just die.

Nonarc - So what's happening in (())-land?

(()) - Nothing much. I just got up, myself. Goin to work pretty soon. And on that note, talk to you later!

Nonarc- Later.

Man what a great conversation that was.

Still like 15 minutes till class starts.

_____ - Hahh.

I sigh and rest my head on the desk.

I hear someone walk in and sit down at the desk in front of me.

It’s Steve. He doesn’t say anything, so I don’t either.

*Ding Ding Ding*

The bell rings signaling the beginning of class.

Instructor - “Alright! Everybody quiet down as I take attendance.”

The instructor calls over the din of mindless high school chatter.

I hear one last laugh made loudly over the rest of the receding conversation.

Shut the hell up already. What’s so fucking funny. It was probably just a dumb comment, or some stupid joke that means nothing. You probably don’t even find it funny. You’re probably just laughing to fill the expected social role.

...and that's uh. That.

If you're curious the username "nonarc" was a reversal of my xbox live gamertag and go-to username at the time "cranon".

Moving swiftly on.
