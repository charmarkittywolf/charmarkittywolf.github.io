Who is this girl?

I am Charlotte-Marie.

My original dream was to become a psychologist but I had to abandon it because I couldn't afford the schooling.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

My heart has screamed out to me my whole life that I am a girl but the government and culture I live under wishes to eradicate my ability to live that truth because I was born with a penis.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

I've been sexually harassed multiple times due to simply being a girl, and have once been sexually predated upon. Most girls that I have talked to about this topic have experienced comparable things their whole lives.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

I wish to do good in the world. I wish to live in a just culture and society. But there is no reasonable way to meaningfully affect change in a world in which the incentive structure is built around profit and output. The systems of suffering and exploitation that run our world are inescapable and inevitable.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

I've always wished to give birth to and raise my own children, but I will never be able to do that due to my anatomy.

Additionally, even if I wished to use a surrogate, the prospect of bringing a new life into a world built off of slavery, misogyny, and exploitation feels morally unacceptable.

And even if I wanted to adopt, it would require an improvement to my current economic situation that I do not see a path towards attaining.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

I lack the willingness to become a slave to another for the sake of earning money. Without money I am prohibited from meeting my wants and needs, and exist as a burden on Kristian. This dynamic actively hurts my relationship with him, in spite of us loving each other.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

In spite of a strong desire to problem solve and remain optimistic, no amount of scheming or commitment or belief has resulted in any ability to meaningfully address or change the previous problems.

This fact burdens my soul and makes me resent the world I am part of.

But.

I am still here.

I have love.

My soul still persists beneath the weight of the world.

Surely, there must be enough left of my spirit.

Enough to at least move an inch while all these things burden me.

I want to believe I have more of my inner light to share.

That these burdens are not enough to completely eradicate my strength.

I wanted to be a psychologist to help people. I can't let my powerlessness consume that light inside of me.

I am Charlotte-Marie.

I was here.
