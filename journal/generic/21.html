I Want A Master

October 4th, 2026

I want somebody to take control of my life.

I want somebody with ambition to see the unpolished potential in me, grab me, use me, train me, and mold me into somebody capable of helping them achieve their goals.

I want to devote myself fully and utterly to somebody I feel is worthy of what I am sure I am capable of.

I can't motivate myself. I just don't feel like doing things that aren't fun, regardless of wether or not I 'should'. But if there was somebody willing of just... Taking me in. Seeing me. Disciplining me. Understanding my needs and taking charge of me. Somebody to organize my life and orient me towards the process of living with structure and purpose...

I can't give myself those things. I can't come up with my own structure and purpose, because it's all fake. None of it's real. Anything I come up with is as empty and transient as the last thing. I have no real passion, I have no real drive, I just want to be praised, damn it!

That's all it's ever been. I want praise, I want acknowledgment! I don't actually give a shit about expressing myself as much as I give a shit about BEING SEEN and BEING PRAISED.

But Idk how to acheive that. I'm not passionate enough about anything to force myself into any sort of purusit or skill or community.

The 100 Things List is helping, but idk, it's not enough for me to feel like every day is worth living. That the maintenance required by the process of living is somehow worth all the effort.

Dishes, Laundry, Money Mangement, Cleaning, Organizing. I just can't fucking be bothered to give a shit.

I feel obligated to help Kristian and that's enough sometimes but it doesn't exactly fill me with a sense of joy and purpose or anything.

What the fuck is Kristian doing with his life other than spend all day on the computer anyway.

What do I do all this for? To die one day? Why am I here? What gives me purpose? Why should I even bother?

I want somebody I respect to give me that for me. To trust in somebody to take care of me, and to use me effectively for their own desires, whims, and ambition.

God that's all I so desperately want.

But I suppose I'll continue to try my pathetic best whenever I can, even without a master, in the vain hope that one day somebody will see me and make me their servant familiar.

...and also cause I'm still here anyway. Master or not. I'm not so motivationless as to always just be laying in bed fantasizing about being somebody's precious little kitten.

...as if such a conveneniently put-together and well-meaning person who matches my specific desires and needs would be so easily found by me anyway.

Fuck me and fuck you and fuck life. It's so stupid.

I have two and a half (Sorry Emi that I'm counting you as half while you're lost beyond the Georgian Veil) loving partners and I still act all miserable because nobody wants to take responsibility for me like I'm a child or a pet.

But hey the heart wants what the heart wants I guess. I can't control this just as much as I can't control feeling like a girl.

Some part of me identifies as a familiar without a master, longing for the day I gain a collar. And this has been true for a while now.

It's such a strong part of me that it felt important to immortalize on my website, even if its locked behind a forbidden-lock.

I Want A Master.

And some part of me will keep seeking for one until I either find them or die.
