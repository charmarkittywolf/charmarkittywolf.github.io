Sexual Desire as Shadow Processing

June 30th, 2026

Pretty much immediately after my crash out, Kristian helped create a plan to maybe get me an orchiechtomy anyway, by getting passports and getting it done in thailand or something while visiting his dad.

His unwavering support helped make me feel better and be less catastrophic about everything.

Additionally, I went to a BDSM munch which helped me to continue to think a lot about sex and I"ve come to the conclusion that my main issue is really not having T flowing through me, but more... a lack of healhty engagement with my sexual desire. (Which, duh, obvious, but I was having a hard time understanding the mechanics.)

My theory is that sexual desire can work as an effective way to process our (Jungian) shadows. It's a way for us to connect with and process parts of ourselves that we otherwise don't acknolwedge or identify with. Or express parts of ourselves that normally can't be accepted by society.

But I wasn't engaging with it with the understanding that it could be such a therapeutic thing. I was engaging with it specifically as a form of self destruction.

If I think of it less as a permanent corrupting and destruction of my soul, and instead as a temporary indulgence that helps me connect with myself, I'm suddenly no longer motivated to do the really awful things, and instead am just doing sort of indulgent, perhaps embarassing or lessering things that more accurately reflect the parts of myself I don't normally allow myself to express.

This goes further than the self-harm stuff too. My engagement with BDSM has historically been pretty rocky and I think this is because I wasn't treating it as the temporary releif it should be, but instead was enamored with "lifestyle" changes. Permanent engagements with dynamics that inherently stripped me of my agency.

I was destroying myself by letting my shadow consume me whole, instead of simply indulging in the taboo and illicit as a releif like a normal person.

So uh... yeah. Character Arc concluded.

I didn't quite write my thoughts down as effectively as I'd like, but for now it's good enough. Thanks for coming to my TED talk, future me/freak who's reading my more personal journal entries.
