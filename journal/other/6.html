I've been connecting with my spiritual side lately. And part of those explorations led to me seriously engaging with and considering The Christian God.

That if you have to use Christianity to connect with your inner voice. To connect with that sacred, holy, human feeling of "God", then who am I to judge?

Yknow those sorts of milktoast vanilla perspectives.

I know someone who has suffered at the hands of a family member and was taught to process her abuse by turning to God? The same god who then allowed her child to be sexually assualted? And she's supposed to find salvation through him somehow?

Even though that same god teaches us to forgive her and her childs abusers.

Even though that same god preaches a philosophy that supports her family's desicion to downplay her abusers crimes and tells her to forgive and get over it.

And this is not an isolated case. It doesn't take a genius to be able to understand the connection between the mormon faith and her suffering. Their are countless, numerous documented cases of the LDS church downplaying and covering up sexual abuse.

As such, I condemn Christianity in its entirety.

The degree to which I am infuriated by my prior "well-meaning" perspective that treated unconditional forgiveness as somehow a good thing, rather than as a tool for those in power to justify abuse of those weaker than them cannot be understated.

We live in a society which conditions men to be cowards incapable of taking accountability for anything. Which conditions them to forgive themselves and accept a world filled with evil rather than reflect and improve themselves and fight against that world.

We live in a society which cultivates weakness and cowardice. A world where men like the one depicted in Obsession take advantage of women simply because they are too fucking scared to face a harsh reality, and its a direct result of this fucking hypocritical cowardly christian bullshit thats used to both demand obedience from the weak in one hand and forgive their abusers in the other.

A culture of victim-blaming, of refusing to self-reflect, of refusing to change.

A culture that neccessarily results in a rise of christian nationalism.

Of fascism.

And if you are made uncomfortable by this fact good! You should be! But don't let that discomfort make you look away from the truth of it all! Don't give into the cowardice that so many others have!

The world is wretched! All that we were taught to worship and venerate contributes directly to the suffering of those that are different or weak! And we must live our best lives in spite of that! So don't turn away! Embrace this world! Fight evil where you can!

God is dead. Everything is permissible. We are condemned to freedom.

Choose love. Not cowardice.
