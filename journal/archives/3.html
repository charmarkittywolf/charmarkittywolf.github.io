A Document and Stream of Thought

Two days after Christmas, half a year after I graduated from High School I wrote both of these documents. I think I took a year off before starting college because my grandma died? I honestly don't remember. This period, and the period where I was in Texas State are the hardest periods for me to recall from my life.

December 27th, 2015

Draft 1 of The Document

I give you my greetings, unknown reader of this document. Whether you be a future version of myself or perhaps a close friend that I have decided should get to know me better, I wrote this document for you to better understand many things about myself.

First. The most important thing to understand about myself is change. Change. I am always constantly changing. One of the very very few things that never change about me is that I am constantly changing. What I believe strongly on one day may very well be the exact opposite of what I believe the next. This is in fact, a very common occurrence.

I am very easily influenced. Far too easily in fact. And quite possibly because of this natural tendency, I have built up natural mental resistances to my own emotions and tendencies. However I don’t always use them. The type of mental state I have at a given moment has a lot to do with what I allow myself to think, and what I believe myself to genuinely feel. But now I’m just kind of rambling. Let’s get my thoughts better organized.

The point is that not only am I constantly changing my philosophy, way of thinking, and attitude towards life, but I am at almost all times regardless of my current mental state incredibly, painfully, aware of the fact that I am incredibly likely to have a completely different mental state the following day. At times I am perfectly fine with this. At others I desperately wish not to change in spite of knowing with full certainty that I will.

Perhaps, if you are not myself reading this document, at this point you are skeptical. Perhaps to you, I have always been the same. You would be right. I usually act the same way around a specific person regardless of my mental state. There might be slight variations. I might say less one day, or be more likely to speak my mind on certain days more than others. But I generally use a personality that is mostly consistent. Why? Well this has partially to do with how others see me. Once I have an established role I subconsciously stick to it. With most of my friends, I am that one zany Otaku fellow. That is how I feel I am seen. That is how I act. That is what I believe myself to be. But that’s hardly how I feel at other times. In fact it is not uncommon for me to despise many anime-related conventions. The concept of “moe” being a particularly hit or miss concept for me to like at one time and despise at another. And now we are starting to arrive at the crux of the problem. Is how I act with my friends genuine? Or not? Do I act in a certain way simply to appease their image of me? Who am I? What is it I want? What is it I value? Sometimes I can answer this question. Others I cannot. But the fact is that even if I do have an answer it will still almost certainly change in the future. Does my current way of thought even hold meaning when it can change on a whim?

A Stream of Thought

I have a mental state that I like to describe as “inconsistent”. What I believe at one time to be true, I will believe at another to be false. What I want at one time, I will work to destroy at another. The type of person I strive to be will end up being the type of person I despise most and vice versa. I have, in the past, hated humanity and myself. I have, in the past, had a lot of hope for humanity and myself. At the time of writing this, I find that I have no hope or hatred. Things are what they are. Because my opinion depends on the moment and is so varied, I don’t care enough to establish one. It will definitely change anyway. I act how I feel I should act in a certain moment. Does this mean living up to other people’s expectations of me? Is that how I want to be? Is that me? Who am I? Enjoyment. Satisfaction. Friends. Love. Is that what I want? Is that all I need? I am here. I will not be here in the future. I was not here in the past. Nothing Matters.

...and that's as far as I got.
